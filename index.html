Picnic math

A picnic planned by vibes ends up three people sharing one corner of the blanket, a feast for twelve packed for four, or one lonely ice pack guarding a whole cooler. Picnic math turns the blanket and a labeled spot into the seating grid, turns the party and a labeled portion into the boxes to pack, and turns the cooler and a labeled pack coverage into the ice. Exact arithmetic, every borrowed number labeled.

Open the calculators

The three tools

  • Spot plan - the blanket at a labeled spot becomes the seating grid and the fringe left over.
  • Basket plan - the party at a labeled portion becomes the food total and the boxes to pack.
  • Ice plan - the cooler at a labeled pack coverage becomes the ice packs and the overlap.

Labeled norms

Labeled norms: a 60 cm sitting spot, a 500 g portion, a 1200 g packing box, 10 L of cooler per ice pack - all illustrative defaults you can change. Real picnics depend on weather, park rules, appetites and food safety guidance. This is planning arithmetic, not an outing guarantee: pack for the real day.
